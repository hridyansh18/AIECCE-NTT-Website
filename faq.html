Frequently asked questions

Who can join the 1 year NTT?

The prospectus lists High School (10th) or equivalent as the qualifying examination.

Can I join the 2nd year directly?

Lateral entry is listed for Intermediate (+2) or equivalent with a one-year N.T.T. from a registered institute.

What languages are available?

Teaching is in Hindi, English, Punjabi and regional languages. Question papers are in Hindi and English only.

Are the accessories compulsory?

No. They are optional and sold at cost to maintain uniformity.

Can I transfer to another branch?

The prospectus says all-India transfers are available at no extra charge.

Does this form complete my admission?

No. It is an enquiry. The institute will contact you about the next steps.

Ready to start your teaching career?

Send an enquiry and our team will contact you.
