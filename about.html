About the institute

Training future nursery teachers with practical, job-oriented education.

Who we are

SR NTT Teacher Training Institute

We offer Nursery Teacher's Training in Early Childhood Care and Education (E.C.C.E). Our courses follow the AIECCE prospectus and are designed so that students can start teaching, or open their own pre-primary class, after completing the programme.

AIECCE (All India Early Childhood Care & Education) has its head office in New Delhi, and the prospectus lists Indira Gandhi School of Education as collaborator.
