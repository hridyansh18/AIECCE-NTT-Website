Admission

Check eligibility, follow four simple steps and send your enquiry online.

Eligibility

Who can apply

  • 1 Year NTT: High School (10th) or equivalent.
  • Lateral entry to 2nd year: Intermediate (+2) or equivalent, plus a one-year N.T.T. (5 or 7 papers) from a registered institute or organisation.

Documents

What to keep ready

  • Copies of educational certificates and marksheets
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Prospectus with Admission-cum-Registration form (₹200)
  • Note: photocopies of the admission form are not accepted

Process

How to apply

  1. Send enquirySubmit the online form below.
  2. Get the prospectusCollect the admission form from the institute office for ₹200.
  3. Submit documentsHand in the filled form with certificate copies and photographs.
  4. Pay fees & beginPay as advised by the institute and start your course.
