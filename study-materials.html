Study books

Books written by education experts

Educational experts from various parts of the country have written theory and practical books in Hindi, English, Punjabi and regional languages. They keep the syllabus of Early Childhood Care and Education updated from time to time.

The "A.I.E.C.C.E NTT Manual" gives a comprehensive review of Early Childhood Education in India and covers all topics of pre-primary education so that students grasp the subject clearly.

Good to know

Medium, identity card and transfers

Medium of teaching

Hindi, English, Punjabi and regional languages. Choose your examination medium and mention it in the examination form. Question papers are in Hindi and English only.

Identity card

Every student receives an identity card from AIECCE New Delhi and shows it to the centre-in-charge or teacher.

Free transfers

Transfer between branches at no extra charge by showing the last fee receipt and an application from the parent or guardian.
